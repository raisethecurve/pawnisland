What The First Lesson Covers

  • Your chess background, rating, goals, and upcoming events.
  • A recent game or position that reveals how you currently make decisions.
  • One or two high-leverage training assignments to begin momentum.
  • A recommendation for lesson format, frequency, and study focus.

Why Local Context Matters

Providence players and families often need help choosing the right next step: first rated event, stronger online practice, better tournament habits, or a more serious training cycle. Coaching gives that decision some structure. Instead of guessing what to study next, we use actual games and goals to choose the work.

Students can train online or discuss local Rhode Island options when an in-person lesson makes sense.
