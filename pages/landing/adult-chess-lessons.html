A Better Way To Study As An Adult

Adult players often have plenty of information and not enough direction. The problem is rarely a lack of videos, books, engines, or databases. The problem is deciding which work matters now.

Coaching narrows the field. We use your games to identify the few patterns that cost the most points, then build assignments that fit your time, temperament, and playing goals.

Adult Chess Lesson FAQs

Is it too late to improve?

No. Adult students improve by studying with better focus and reviewing their actual decisions.

What if I mostly play online?

Online games are still useful evidence. Bring them to the lesson and we will extract training themes.

Do I need a rating?

No. A rating helps, but a recent game and clear goals are enough to start.
